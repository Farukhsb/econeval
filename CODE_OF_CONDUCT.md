# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

We expect everyone who contributes, reviews, uses, or discusses this project to act with respect, professionalism, and good faith.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on the merits of ideas and code.
- Assume good intent unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Give and receive feedback clearly and without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Personal attacks, insults, or intimidation
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or contributions
- Sharing private information without permission

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may lead to temporary or permanent restriction from project participation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository owner or GitHub contact channels.

Please include:

- what happened
- who was involved, if known
- when it happened
- any relevant links or screenshots
